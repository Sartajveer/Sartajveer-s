Building planets…
Desktop: drag to look, W/S or scroll to fly, click a planet, keys 0–8 pick a body, +/− speed, H home.
Phone: use the buttons above, or tap Phone look and move your phone; tap to select what’s under the dot.
Headset: point + trigger to fly to a body, touchpad up/down flies where you point, left/right changes time speed, grip returns home.
